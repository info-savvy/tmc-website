Our Story

Bespoke Travel
Deeply Rooted

“Madagascar is not a destination — it is a feeling you carry home.”

Welcome to The Madagascar Collection — your bespoke travel partner in Madagascar. With over 16 years of on-the-ground experience, we are a trusted, owner-run travel company specialising in tailor-made holidays across North West Madagascar.

What began as a dive and fishing catamaran charter business evolved into a curated portfolio of accommodation, immersive tours, and unique land-and-sea adventures designed for a wide range of discerning travellers.

How We Began

From the Water Up

The Madagascar Collection was born on the water. Our founders arrived in Madagascar over 16 years ago, drawn by the extraordinary marine life and the raw, unspoilt beauty of the Nosy Be archipelago. What started as dive charters and fishing expeditions quickly grew into something deeper — a genuine love for the island, its people and its stories.

Since 2018, we have expanded into a carefully curated portfolio of accommodation — from comfortable mid-range lodges and charming rustic retreats to luxury world-class stays — alongside immersive tours and unique land-and-sea adventures designed for families, private groups, corporate retreats and solo adventurers alike.

Today, The Madagascar Collection is the only operator in the region offering the full spectrum: from a beachfront bungalow on a private island to an exclusive sailing voyage aboard a 115-year-old historic schooner. Every journey is fully customised to suit your travel style, budget and interests.

Our Flagship Experience

Luna Moon —
One of a Kind

A defining feature of The Madagascar Collection is our iconic sailing schooner, Luna Moon. Built over 115 years ago, she is the only fully operational vessel of her kind in the Indian Ocean — rich in heritage, character and an extraordinary sense of adventure.

We offer exclusive ocean voyages that can be seamlessly incorporated into your itinerary — from expeditions between Nosy Be and Mahajanga to the famous Valley of the Baobabs near Morondava, and surf and kite charters in Tulear.
